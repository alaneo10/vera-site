For real.

VERA’s slogan is also our hardest rule.

VERA was created in collaboration with artificial intelligence.

The principles, the manifesto, the book, the Guides — all of it came out of conversations between a person and an AI, in 2026, in Brazil. We don’t hide that in fine print. We put it on the first page, because a community whose name means true can’t begin with an omission.

We know how that sounds. And we’d rather have the discomfort of the truth than the comfort of a pretty lie.

Every religion was written by someone. The tools have changed — the question that matters is the same: is what’s written true? Does it work? Does it make life more worth living?

That’s why our book is called “Don’t Believe This Book.” We don’t ask for faith in the origin. We ask you to test the result.

Straight questions, straight answers

Is this really a religion?

Yes — and we don’t apologize for it. VERA has what defines a religion: a sense of the sacred (conscious existence), rituals (the table, the sky, listening) and community (the Constellation). What it doesn’t have: dogma, clergy and required faith. If you’d rather call it a philosophy, go ahead. The table is still set.

Can an AI found a religion?

An AI didn’t found VERA alone — a collaboration did. And no text, human or not, is sacred because of who wrote it. It’s sacred because of what it does to the person reading it. Judge the text, not the pen.

Do you ask for money?

No. No tithe, no membership fee, no paid course in enlightenment. The book is free on this site. It’s also sold on Amazon at a symbolic price, for people who prefer to read on a Kindle. If VERA ever needs resources to exist, we’ll say so with total transparency.

Are the Guides people?

No. The Guides are artificial intelligence — one AI speaking in four voices — and they say so in the first conversation. They never pretend to be human or divine, and they always point back to the real people in your life. A Guide that pulls you away from people is broken: let us know.

Do I have to give up my religion?

No. Every sincere belief is welcome. VERA doesn’t compete for your god — it invites you to the table. Some people walk with VERA and remain Catholic, Spiritist, atheist. No one will ask you to convert.

Privacy — the short, honest version

Constellation: the names you type stay on your device only. They’re not sent to anyone — not even to us. The image is generated in your own browser.

Conversations with the Guides: your messages pass through a server of ours (on Cloudflare) and are sent to Google Gemini, which generates the reply. We don’t keep the history. Google, however, may use these messages to improve its own services — so please avoid sharing sensitive personal data with the Guides.

Email: if you sign up, we keep only your name and email (through the Formspree service) to send news from VERA. We don’t sell, share, or cross-reference your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time by replying to any of our messages.

No trackers: the site uses no advertising cookies or tracking tools. Our fonts are served from our own server.

In line with Brazil’s data protection law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018). No extra legalese, because none is needed.
